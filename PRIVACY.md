# Privacy Policy for Synoetis

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2026  
**Developer:** AtomerX  
**Contact:** https://github.com/athulrajmp21/Synoetis/issues

---

### 1. Overview
Synoetis is an offline-first desktop productivity application built with Rust and Tauri. We believe your personal productivity, academic data, career logs, and habit routines belong strictly to you.

### 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Local Storage Only:** All user data (including tasks, habits, study sessions, academic assessments, and job applications) is stored locally on your device in an embedded SQLite database.
- **No Remote Telemetry:** Synoetis does not track your activity, log keystrokes, collect analytics, or sell your personal information to third parties.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, sign in, or provide personal credentials to use the application.

### 3. Network Access
Synoetis requires standard internet client capability solely for:
- Checking GitHub Releases for application updates (via the built-in auto-updater engine).
- Opening external user-clicked hyperlinks in your default system browser (such as documentation or job postings).

No personal or productivity data is ever transmitted over these connections.

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Because all data resides strictly on your local machine, you have complete control over it:
- You can export, back up, or reset your local database at any time from within the app settings.
- Uninstalling Synoetis removes the application and associated local program files.

### 5. Third-Party Services
Synoetis does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or tracking services.

### 6. Contact & Support
If you have questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on GitHub:
https://github.com/athulrajmp21/Synoetis/issues
